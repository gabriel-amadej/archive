**December 8th, 2013**

When progressives are presented with concepts like freed money, open-source education, open-source production, etc etc., they reflexively step in saying, with a stern voice, "don't hurt yourself", as they politely regulate out any possible means by which these emergent technologies might be used by the disadvantaged to empower themselves. We've seen it already with gun printing: under the false pretense of public safety, they have passionately called for tight regulations, patent lockdowns, and in extreme cases outright bans on the use or certain uses of 3d printing. Here we might draw a parallel to safety code regulations, which effectively ban household production with normal household goods, and ensure that capital-intensive manufacturing is the only competitive force. Here too do regulationist busybodies threaten the future of free and open desktop manufacturing. While they might call themselves "left-wing", they are in practice apologists for the current (and past) state of affairs; they reinforce in our minds the necessity of hierarchy and existing power relations. 'This is the way it has been, and so it shall be'. When they pretend to ally themselves with the Left, they speak falsely—not that they are lieing, although this is sometimes true, but while they don't know 'it'—they do 'it'! And what is 'it' that they do? Everything that they say they are against. Inequality, systemic oppression, unfairness? -- These are paradigmatic concerns for those of us on the Left, at least those of us on the genuine Left, but these progressives have had their hand in enclosing access to production and to the means of self-subsistence since their roots.[1] They say we should raise the minimum wage, but why don't they ever suggest that we abolish all existing absentee legal title to vacant and unimproved land, including all heirs and assigns to those illegitimate titles not gained by actual homesteading, thereby opening the means to self-subsistence for many of those impoverished, with the mere stroke of a pen? Like Franz Oppenheimer argued, labour cannot be exploited when employers have to legitimately compete with viable self-employment. Those of us on the Left who support freed markets, where access to the means of production is not artificially enclosed for an emergent capitalist class, labour wouldn't merely receive a higher wages or the equivalent of a strong minimum wage hike. Labour would instead receive it's natural and just wage: it's full product. But then we wouldn't have capitalism anymore—progressives need capitalism like a parasitic organism needs to sustain the host in order to bleed it ad infinitum.
