**July 30th, 2015**

My mother was a liberal. A certified, convinced, 60s civil rights marching liberal. I was raised on Pete Seeger songs and Martin 
Luther King speeches, on Kennedys, NPR and the Democratic Party, and she would have adored Bernie Sanders. Not just his politics, 
but his attitude, his feistiness, is history, everything. she would've put a sign on our lawn and argued with people about him. He 
very probably would of had a crush on him. Bernie Sanders was my mom's kind of guy.
 
A couple days ago Vox published an interview Ezra Klein did with Sen. Sanders where the senator shared some of his ideas about 
immigration and the best way to describe them is, I think, grotesque. The level of economic silliness, knee-jerk reactionism (I 
don't think "cuz the Koch Brothers it." should generally be considered a decent explanation for the holding of a political point 
of view on the part of a 12-year-old, let alone a senator), and 18th/19th century crypto-racist nativist unionism was kind of 
impressive in its audaciousness.
 
I don't know much about economics but I know that immigration is something the great majority of economists agree on, and they 
agree that it's a positive.The worst, most negative assessment extant is that it may have a temporary effect of -8% on some low 
skill jobs. Long term the market adjusts, but that's it, short term, maybe, worst case scenario, some people think maybe -8% 
temporarily. Almost every study says and almost all economists believe that immigration is a clear, unadulterated positive for the 
economy, but let's put that aside for a minute and pretend that the few dottering cranks who still think immigrants are taking our 
jobs are really smarter than everyone else and not racist at all and that it might have some negative repercussions.

 
Who fucking cares? Why the hell am I supposed to give a shit? I'm supposed to get all worked up and incensed at the Koch brothers 
screwing some white guy out of some of his goddamn vacation days but for some reason I'm just supposed to throw empathy out the 
window if the person is a couple miles further away from me? Is that where we are now? Is that progressivism? Is that liberalism? 
People living in squalor, living on a couple dollars, a few quarters, a day and the best we can do is "Fuck you, I've got mine, 
Pedro."? That's where we're at? Yeah, yeah, I feel bad and all, but if it's going to cost me a dime, forget it?

 
Putting aside a few million Native Americans (because that's how we generally like to deal with them), this is a nation composed 
almost entirely of immigrants, and this is the best we can do? This is what the great, white hope of progressivism (emphasis on 
white, apparently) has to say about people who want to work, want to improve their lives? "Sorry, tired, huddled, poor, don't need 
you gumming up the system anymore. You might interfere with overtime negotiations." Is this where "Workers of the World, Unite?" 
has gone? " Unite, unless, you know, you're from someplace really bad and you really need a job and maybe you're the wrong color." 
From where I'm sitting, it looks to me like Bernie is pretty cool with keeping a substantial portion of the world population in 
their shackles. If that's "progressive", if that's "liberal", then you're going to have to give up your assumed stewardship of the 
moral high ground, lefties. "Getting yours" is not an idealogical position that entitles you to look down your nose at anyone.

 
So yeah, my mother would have loved Bernie Sanders, but my mother was also a liberal and a progressive and a socialist and she 
would not have loved the garbage he's dribbling now. Not at all. She loved the United States, and she always stuck up for the 
little guy, but unlike Sen. Sanders, that included little guys everywhere, not just on the other side of her backyard fence. She 
may have continued to support him after this, but man would she have had some choice words for the senator.
 
Talking about this the last couple days, one trope, one figure of speech, I kept seeing was "Shouldn't we take care of our own, 
first?" or "We need to help ourselves before we help other people." First of all, immigrants come here to work, at a greater rate, 
and harder, than those of us born here. They don't come here looking for a hand out, they don't want Sen. Sanders' help. They want 
to fucking work, and work hard, doing a lot of the things native born Americans don't want to do. But more importantly, much more 
importantly, the real reason I wouldn't care if it cost a couple bucks, is that the people we're talking about are "us", they are 
"our own, they are "our neighbors." That's all. They're "my own" as much as the guy next door and certainly as much as Bernie 
Sanders. A couple wars and land grabs and borders don't signify anything next to our common, shared humanity. I learned that from 
my mother, the liberal, the socialist, the human fucking being.
