**July 27th, 2013**

The very first thing to exist (technically the only thing, but we'll get to that) was the godhead. It was a giant big glob of everything, a sleeping and dreaming godhead. It is unaware of itself, and cannot perceive its own existence. In its sleep, it dreamed, and the dream it came up with was Anu, the Everything, or simply IS. However, there must always be antithesis, so at the same time, Anu implied and thus created the existence of its other, Padomay, the Nothing, or simply IS NOT.

These are two dualistic yet mutually contradictory concepts. And they interacted with each other. Anu is the Everything, but it is the reaction of Padomay to it that creates the essence of something, and so Padomay is the prime force, the prime concept behind all things. However, remember that these are concepts, ideas. They are not beings. They are also subgradiented - they are a lesser form of the Godhead that is Everything in the dream.

Anyway, IS/IS NOT reacted against one another, and as a reaction there were two things spawned. Anui-El (Stasis) and Sithis (Change). The degree to which these things are separate from Anu/Padomay is arguable, but regardless, they are the "soul" of Anu and Padomay respectively. Anui-El is the everything, and Sithis is the nothing that thus forms the reaction. Think of it in terms of sound.

Anui-El (and Anu) is sound. All sound, everywhere, forever. This means it is just a giant wall of noise. By adding in gaps, spaces where there is no sound, you get music, and that is what Sithis does. It changes to create. You need both.

Anyway, this "music" from the two was spawned as first the personification of Time. Bormahu, Aka, Akatosh, Aka-Tusk, Auri-El, Alkosh, whatever. Then there were all the other forces/beings who were spawned. Mephala, Hermaeus Mora, Mara, Magnus Zenithar, Trinimac, Azura, and all the others. The et'Ada, original spirits.

Remember, these are all further subgradiented down from Anu and Padomay, and the Godhead. Now we come to Lorkhan. He's a tricky one. Thartaag the World-Devourer, the Greedy Man, Lorkhan, Shor, Sep, Sheor, and so on. He is a Padomaic spirit, who in his first moment of existence, spied the Tower, the Eternal I, and wanted it. So he convinced all the other et'Ada to prevent subgradients from happening (all of the Aurbis goes downward, never up), with a giant machine-magic thing. This was Mundus.

The et'Ada sacrificed of themselves to create it. Literally. They died as part of the contract of creation, and exist as plane(t)s in the sky. They are essence, the Earth Bones of the Wheel (and turn a wheel sideways, and you get an "I"). Not all of them chose to do so. In the Altmeri worldview, the ones who sacrificed were the Aedra, their Ancestors (I'll get to why in a moment). The ones who did not sacrifice were the Daedra, Not-Ancestors. Now, this is where things get tricky. Up until this point, time had been non-linear, the Dawn Era. So let's keep on going.

In the Dawn Era, everything happened at once. The Ur-Tower and Convention. However, perhaps because of the betrayal that was seen as the creation of Mundus (i.e. the et'Ada became bound), Auri-El and Trinimac (mostly Trinimac) hunted down Lorkhan, who was the architect of this, and Trinimac knocked him down and ripped out his Heart. These are the wars of the Ehlnofey, the god-ancestors who subgradiented down from the et'Ada who sacrificed to form Creation, the Mundus. Make sure this is in your mind: the Ehlnofey are the ancestors of all races on Nirn except for the Argonians/Hist. Some of the Ehlnofey are the Wandering Ehlnofey (humans, for example), while others were the Old Ehlnofey (the Mer races and Khajit).

Now, where was I? Ah yes. The Heart of Lorkhan was the Heart of the World, and it could not be destroyed so easily. So it was shot off over the ocean, and it eventually came to land in what is now Vvardenfell. The landing created Red Mountain, which meant Red Tower. Remember what I said about music above? Well, time was non-linear until Red Tower happened. Red Tower and the First Stone, the Heart of Lorkhan. With one tower (Ur-Tower and Zero Stone), you had one beat. With two (adding Red Tower and the Heart of Lorkhan), you have two beats, and two beats make a rhythm. This is partly why Lorkhan is known as the Doom Drum among the Altmer. Thus, time began to happen in linear motion, and the Merethic Era began, where the Mer spread out (after they subgradiented down from the Ehlnofey into the distinct races we've got now, though they hadn't entirely departed from the Aldmer yet into Altmer, Falmer, Bosmer, Dwemer, Chimer and so on). As they spread out, they constructed new Towers in imitation of the old. Tree-Sap, Orichalc, White-Gold, Snow, and so on and so forth.

Also note that all life began on Tamriel. The Ehlofey left, and then returned after subgradienting down into humans and so on.

And, again, remember that everything is going down. The Altmer see the creation of Mundus as a prison, because they were no longer one with the Aedra, their literal ancestors via subgradience. To go back to the pre-Dawn state, what is required is to re-combine. Picture a glass of water. Pour half into another glass. Then pour half of the two you have into two more. And so on and so forth. That is subgradience, continually going down from the whole. Everything is still a tiny fraction of what came before, but it is no longer with that greater whole.

This led to a number of attempts to re-reach the sacred, to create it from the deaths of the profane (as the Dwemer would say). There were a few methods attempted. The Falmer had a working one, a personalised relationship that is demonstrated in the "Touching the Sky" book. The Aldmer developed one that involved keeping elder magic bound to try and keep it from degenerating. The Dwemer attempted Numidium, as a means of attempted brute-forcing their way to the Godhead by sacrificing everybody in their race and thus powering Transcendence.

There are other methods - the Scarab that becomes the New Man, for example. The Enantiomorph. However, these are all collectively known as the Walking Ways. They are a means of breaking free of the "trap" that is Mundus, and re-reaching the Divine. The process of CHIM is one such method, one Walking Way, and it is Vivec's preferred methodology. It involves recognition of the fact that all the Mundus and life itself is, in fact, a dream. It is the dream of a sleeping godhead in an attempt to understand and perceive itself, something it can never do. Realising that you are a part of the dream, however, means that you do not exist. You are a figment of the imagination.

You Are Not.

However, that isn't the end of things. If you can take the knowledge, and yet love yourself and your existence so much that you can resist it, you can look the entirety of Everything in the face and say the opposite.

No, I Am.

By doing that, holding these two contradictory elements within yourself, you can return to the first brush of Anu and Padomay. You reside within your Tower, the Eternal I, and have achieved CHIM. You have gone back upstream, defying the eternal subgradience, and become one with the Godhead. You perceive both yourself, as the Ruling King, and you perceive the Godhead. You are, for all intents and purposes, omnipotent.

Also realise that this is not a matter of logic, because logic and the entire universe is saying that no, you don't exist. You quite literally can vanish in a puff of logic (several Moth Priests have done so, seeing the zero-sum, or +1 and -1 put together equaling zero, as the highest form of oneness with everything), unless you have the sheer force of spirit, the purest faith, to deny it and achieve your Tower. It's a fine line to walk. Lose the knowledge, and you go back to sleep. Lose the faith, and you vanish from reality forever. This is why CHIM, the Ehlnofex sigil, is constantly shifting and changing, prone to coming apart with the slightest break in concentration.

This is flawed, of course - there is a downside. If you perceive yourself as a totality, you tend to become detached from it. There is no need to do anything, because you are everything. Why did Talos not just end the Oblivion Crisis personally? Because he is Everything. Do you cut off your left hand because it currently annoys your right? No, of course you don't. It's just a part of you, like everything else is.

Now, this isn't taking you all the way. Nor does CHIM mean you can never "lose a fight" (for lack of a better term). After all, Talos is "more powerful" than Vivec, yet they are both Ruling Kings, and the Ruling King who perceives in another his equal rules nothing. And, of course, Talos has the Enantiomorphic relationship on his side as well, which is another Walking Way.

But there is one final step. The ending stage, final point. It's called Z, the Amaranth. It is where the things you dream wake up, they wail knowing free will, and become dreamers yourselves. It is a new universe. You become your own Godhead, but not only do you perceive yourself, you perceive your children, who perceive both themselves and you, who perceive yourself and them, and they perceive you, and it continues onward forever throughout eternity. It is the ultimate goal of everything to reach that place. And nobody has achieved the Amaranth yet. If CHIM is a selfish love (the ultimate selfishness), then the Amaranth, I have heard it compared to, is the love of a parent for their children.

So that's CHIM. Becoming a Ruling King is interesting. The Daedra Princes know of it, for example - Vivec gained the word CHIM from Molag Bal. They share a relationship to the Ruling King that is equitable to the relationship of royalty, of Princes and Kings, but cannot achieve it.
