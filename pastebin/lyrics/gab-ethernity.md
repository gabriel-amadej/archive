#### GAB'S ROUGH LYRICAL ANALYSIS OF SYBREED'S "ETHERNITY"

[LIVE LINK](https://pastebin.com/ytLxeYmd) --/////-- 
[ARCHIVE LINK](https://archive.ph/NObxo) 

**May 21st, 2012**

.

"This cold morning is like a consecration"

~Describes the sacred-esque feeling of what reality is like upon waking up on a nice morning. It may also described the sacred-esque nature of reality or the experiencing of it.

"A procession of souls that will never end"

~Reality is eternal, and so inevitably more souls will come about - like ours has through a long series of biological evolution and development. Thus, reality is an endless procession of souls.

"The time of remembrance and disillusions
Shall dry the liquid pain running in my veins"

~It implies that the first lines of the song were too naive or a mystical-esque interpretation of reality. Thus, he is disillusioned at this time of rememberance. However, that also gives him strength to face up to a cold, careless reality.

"Drain my blood and drown me in the sun,
forever cured and sanctified
Now reborn, we cross the Rubicon,
Eternity will blend our minds in one."

The end of the verse that comes just previous to the chorus here could be acting as a precursor. This chrous seems to be daydreamy-like. However, I think "eternity will blend our minds in one" is meant as a statement as fact, and contradicts any excessively spiritual and/or religious interpretations of what will follow.

"I close my eyes and shiver, resurrection
Standing on this ground of awakening"

~He knows that reality isn't a hyperinflated, hypermystical ideal, which is troublesome for closing your eyes and mystifying reality in your mind. (This because, "standing on this ground of AWAKENING")

"I float into the light of revelation
'Expire, and thus revive in the purest form'"

~This seems to imply that he sometimes suceeds in temporary delusion, and the last line quoted here seems to be a quote. It could be in the form of a hallucination, or this section could be a mockery of people who believe in this concept in all actuality and seriousness.

"I watch the sky and pray for my deliverance
An endless whisper to find redemption
To breath the fire of all creation divine, purified"

He prays for deliverance to an ideal, however he describes this as an *endless* whisper to find redemption. An endless whisper implies that what he is hoping and searching for has never and never will come. 'To breathe the fire of all creation divine, purified' is another ideal; a form of potential redemption that will never come, as he admits.

"Ad Aeternam... Gloria"

A description of the eternality of the universe. This cannot be an ideal, false description of our souls because as I mentioned earlier, one of the first lines in the songs contradict this outright.
