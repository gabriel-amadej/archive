#### GAB CORRECTS THE LYRIC ERRORS IN SYBREED'S "ANTARES" AND "PULSE"

[LIVE LINK](https://pastebin.com/tJgHenWH) --/////-- 
[ARCHIVE LINK](https://archive.ph/LLpAp) 

**January 28th, 2013** 

.

This is a list of lyric errors in the Sybreed booklets and on allegedly trustworthy lyric sites, but only from Antares and Pulse. 

To explain the format, I quote the false lyric first and then the correction to follow it. 

I've also sorted and numbered them by their default track order on their respective discs. 

You'll notice that some of the corrections are grammatically incorrect or less correct, and that's because that is how he actually sings it.

.

**Antares:**

.

_#1- Emma-0_

-"I'm enabled to 'avoiding' stare at your halo of desperation," really says, "I'm enabled to avoid to stare at your halo of desperation."

.

_#2- Ego Bypass Generator_

-"You blame 'them' all to never trust your lies," says, "You blame us all to never trust your lies."

.

_#3- Revive My Wounds_

-"Irritated by your 'selfish smile'," says, "Irritated by your vicious mind."

-"The wage of 'sins' is to fall in self-destructive schemes," says, "The wage of sin is to fall in self-destructive schemes."

-"Makes me nonexistent and dead inside," says, "Makes me 'so' nonexistent and dead inside."

.

_#4- Isolate_

-"I have no purpose or glorious 'goals'," says, "I have no purpose or glorious goal."

-"Scattered in a wind of 'pestilence'," says, "Scattered in a wind of misery."

-"I've lost all 'hope or' faith," just says, "I've lost all faith."

-"'A' wish to not share the pain," says, "I wish to not share the pain."

-"I desecrate the foundations of my own existence," says, "I desecrate the foundations of my own 'fragile' existence."

-"'I'm lost' into the purest darkness," says, "I run into the purest darkness."

-"I suffocate 'under' the weight of grief and deception," says,  "I suffocate underneath the weight of grief and deception."

.

_#5- Dynamic_

-"Everyday, 'this' cold suffering," says, "Everyday is cold suffering."

-"I'm caught into a dynamic of 'hate'," says, "I'm caught into a dynamic of pain."

-"Like a relentless movement leading to our 'perdition'," says, "Like a relentless movement leading to our damnation."

-"All I could do will never clear the veil before 'our' eyes," says, "All I could do will never clear the veil before your eyes."

.

_#6- Neurodrive_

-"'Electric neurodrive' to induce self-demise," says, "I lack the neurodrive to induce self-demise."

-"The sequence is' accomplished', my 'mind' is now rewired," says, "The sequence is now complete, my brain is now rewired."

.

_#8- Permafrost_

-"I've 'buried' my joy and faith," says, "I bury my joy and faith."

-"And thus 'I've found' a way to purify my mind from desolation," says, "And thus I find a way to purify my mind from desolation."

-"'When' everything was done," says, "If everything was done."

-"The barrel of gun against my head," says, "The barrel of 'a' gun against my head".

-"To live is to die with no 'absolution'," says, "To live is to die with no resolution."

-"'I bury' my ego and 'choke' my hopes," says, "I've buried my ego and choked my hopes."

-"I've 'cursed' myself for this foretaste of ruins or just a glimpse of light," says, "I've 'cured' myself for this foretaste of ruins or just a glimpse of light."

-"I've discovered 'my own' mortality," just says, "I've discovered mortality."

.

_#10- Twelve Megatons Gravity_

-"You're 'scared' of your needs of violence," says, "You're scarred of your needs of violence."

-"All celebrate the 'reign' of pigs, leisure, and atrocity," says, "All celebrate the reigns of pigs, leisure, and atrocity."

-"And enjoy the bitter taste of watching all 'delusions' fade away," says, "And enjoy the bitter taste of watching all delusion fade away."

-"And still you keep on 'denying' the pain," says, "And still you keep on, deny the pain."

-"The 'burnt' of profound misery," just says, "The burn of profound misery."

-"A slaughtered 'heaven', a place of 'fool's' sanity," maybe says, "A slaughtered haven, a place of false sanity."

-"Extracting all corrupted words with the 'precision' of a gun," says, "Extracting all corrupted words with the 'massive strain' of a gun."

.

_#11- Ethernity_

-"I close my eyes and shiver 'in' resurrection," just says, "I close my eyes and shiver, resurrection."

.

_#B- Plasmaterial_

-"We are used to 'swallowing' the poison," says, "We are used to swallow the poison."

-"To close our eyes as we 'descent'," says, "To close our eyes as we descend."

-"A 'fool's' promise to break our heart," says, "A false promise to break our heart."

-"We hide injuries 'deep inside'," just says, "We hide injuries".

-"To lead our steps on this 'ground'," says, "To lead our steps on this floor."

.

_#B- Technocracy_

-"They bring the 'string' of full security," is at least, "They bring the strain of full security," but possibly, "They bring the strain of 'false' security."

-"'Just' watching the ground," actually says, "We're watching the ground."

-"'Could' we shine again?," says, "Can we shine again?"

-"Pain is breeding together, 'together' we remain defenseless," actually says, "Pain is breeding together, we remain defenseless."

.

**The Pulse of Awakening:**

_#1- Nomenklatura_

-"'We have' nothing to preserve," says, "We've got nothing to preserve."

-"We are destined to be 'expandable' products," says, "We are destined to be exploitable products."

.

_#2- A.E.O.N._

-"I am 'a' hollow face who 'controls by delusions'," says, "I am the hollow face who control vile delusion." Lyric sites usually write the grammatically correct version of the corrected line.

-"'Twisting' the soul of men," says, "And bend the soul of men." Lyric sites usually correct the second line but write a grammatically correct version, which is not what he actually sings.
-"Ignorance 'must' prevail," says, "Ignorance shall prevail." Lyric sites usually correct this.

.

_#3- Doomsday Party_

-"And this is how we realize the tragic 'prize' of wasting time," says, "And this is how we realize the tragic price of wasting time."

-"And we'll know in the end if we truly 'live'," says, "And we'll know in the end if we truly lived."

.

_#4- Human Black Box_

-"To get rid of 'such' misery," says, "To get rid of my misery."

-"We support blasphemy 'and' bless hypocrisy," says, "We support blasphemy, then bless hypocrisy."

.

_#6- I Am Ultraviolence_

-"I’m prone to destroy 'all' what is standing in my way," just says, "I’m prone to destroy what is standing in my way."

.

_#7- Electronegative_

-"I blame your vain 'deceits' for 'I see' the plain hypocrisy," says, "I blame your vain deceit for I've seen the plain hypocrisy."

-"'Of' concealing my 'thoughts' to gain security," at least says, "In concealing my thought to gain security," but maybe, "In 'cancelling' my thought to gain security."

-"'When' life is but once given," says, "For life is but once given."

-"Become the 'norms' to bound and regulate humankind," just says, "Become the norm to bound and regulate humankind."

.

_#8- In The Cold Light_

-"I can feel but 'regrets'," just says, "I can feel but 'regret'."

-"'My life is such a' letdown," says, "Every joy's turned to letdown."

.

_#9- Lucifer Effect_

-"'Disillusioned yet fascinated' with bitter cruelty," maybe says, "Your delusion is fascinated with bitter cruelty."

.

_#11- Meridian A.D._

-"We chasing the light 'just' wishing to live a little more," says, "We chasing the light, wishing to live a little more."

-"We rise to 'better' fall," says, "We rise to bitter fall."

.

_#12- From Zero To Nothing_

-"How could I break this 'steady' cycle to rise instead into the light again?," maybe says, "How could I break this deadly cycle to rise instead into the light again?"
