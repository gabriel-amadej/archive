#### GAB'S LYRICAL ANALYSIS OF SYBREED'S "LUCIFER EFFECT"

[LIVE LINK](https://pastebin.com/AMV65gHP) --/////-- 
[ARCHIVE LINK](https://archive.ph/qG4dn) 

**May 20th, 2012**

----------------------------------------------------------

"Disillusioned, yet fascinated"

~Describes the armchair treatment of Christianity by most contemporary Christians who still have an intense, inner piety in some sense.

.

"With bitter cruelty, you deny the sancticty of flesh."

~The Christian indictment of things like sexual expression is bitter, cold, and cruel. They 'deny the sancitity of flesh' both in that sense, and in the sense that they are tend to be too concerned with the spiritual, and an afterlife that does not exist.

.

"Urging me to destroy."

~The observations of Christian behaviour can cause disgust, anger, and the wish to remove it's disease from the face of the earth.

.

"You say it's not a question of trust, the strongest don't lie."

~Describes how Christianity has been a tool for false religious authority and control over the massess for the past two thousand years.

.

"No matter if I have to bleed my own kind, it's all right"

~This power is used and abused to justify unjustifiable wars, and, from a biblical point of view, the slaughter of people who do minor crimes. It also describes the system of repentence inherent to most forms of Christianity; no matter what shit I do, everything will be alright in the end for me.

.

"Like Lucifer rising from the ashes of grace

The downfall of the sun

My heart is frozen

Like Lucifer rising from the ashes of grace

The downfall of the sun

My wings are burning down."

~An antares-like description of how all of this makes him feel. I share this attitude as well.

.

"Oh doppelganger, I hear you in my head whispering, 'What a joke. Their so called faith and beliefs are but filth. Give up all tolerance."

~This refers to his conflict between his hatred of Christianity and the hypocrisy of christians, and his belief in tolerance. It also accurately describes the ugly nature of Christianity.

.

"They loath lust and depravity but ignore their own sins."

~The hypocrisy of christians.

.

"When zeitgeist extols raw pandemonium

Bring them hell"

~This inner conflict between tolerance v. hatred of things disgusting and evil brings him to give into the doppelganger described above. All of the quotes starting from "What a joke" to now are what the doppelganger says in the context of the songer. However, that does not mean it doesn't accuratly describe some of the lyricists active attitudes and beliefs toward and about Christianity.

.

"Prepare yourself to be deceived by those you entrust your very dreams or darkest fears; to be double crossed."

The power Christianity holds over people, resembled through things like the promises it makes, to the level of confessing your entire life to a priest, sways people into trusting their authority. However, in the end, you will be crossed by the people you are entrusting so much of yourself to. This can also be interpretated as a vague reference to or motivated by things like children molesting priests, as the religious parents typically trust "men of God".

.

"There is no place for hope in your world."

~An accurate description of the Christian worldview.

.

"You love to deride my sustained endeavour to find a way to stand still."

~I believe this refers to the stagnation Christianity implicitly encourages today (explicitly in the past) both philosophically, scientifically, and on an existential level as well.
