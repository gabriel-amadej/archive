**February 2nd, 2013** 

.

I used to struggle to change it all

To get rid of my misery

I used to believe it should be easy

To end all suffering

.

It always looks the same:

A ghastly place for pigs and mundane depravity

We support blasphemy then bless hypocrisy

To finally vote for destruction

.

Empires keep on falling

No one can stop the earth to keep on turning by the way

No need to try to fight it, just wait and see

The virtuos liars won't forever win

.

I ran full circle then realized

It was much better to take a seat

And watch this gruesome tragedy

With all your stone and bricks

.

No matter our willpower or might

We have no means to define the very course of fate

It's nothing but delusion, no one can change what lies inside

No one can purge the wrong side of mankind

The virtuous liars are destined to die as well

Here lies the fairness of this life
